BAMpiro QC

Short-read bacterial / MTBC cohort QC. Review flagged samples, tick any to drop, then export keep_list.txt / exclusion.tsv. Thresholds below are live; the pipeline gate itself is unchanged.

Executive summary - cohort health and headline findings at a glance

Live thresholds & presets - adjust and everything re-flags

General statistics - tick a box to basket a sample for exclusion; click a sample name for its full profile; a header to sort

columns
metric help

Flagged samples - to review; the margin is shown under each flag; click a flag to filter the cohort

Per-lineage summary - medians per lineage; # MIXED = samples with >1 lineage above the mixture cut-off; click a row to filter

Distributions - one mark per sample; shaded band = acceptable range (modern gate); hover for detail

PASSWARNFAILbeeswarm dashed line = median

Taxonomic composition - Kraken2 classification of the reads before mapping: the dominant (primary) taxon, the top other taxon, and the unclassified fraction. A low primary % or a large secondary taxon flags possible contamination / a mixed sample.

Correlations - pick two metrics, coloured by QC; drag a box to basket the enclosed samples vs

Metric correlation - Spearman rank correlation across the core metrics, over the samples in view; click a cell to load that pair into the scatter above

QC-metric space - samples ordinated in standardized QC-metric space (PCA on robust z-scores); a descriptive map of how quality profiles co-vary, not phylogeny and not a test

Most unusual samples (robust Mahalanobis rank + top deviating metrics)

Divergence vs completeness - separates reference-bias suspects (low divergence at low missingness) from honest low-coverage (low divergence explained by high missingness); a screen to inspect, not an automatic flag

Consensus completeness - callable / IUPAC / missing per sample, worst first

callableIUPACmissing (- / N)

Genome landscape - a signal along the reference; rows = samples (grouped by lineage). A contiguous block = a localised feature (e.g. an RD deletion, a variant cluster). Missing = consensus callability (coverage proxy); SNPs/Het/Indels = VCF variant density. Drag across the track to zoom a region

none / callable→hightop strip = cohort mean; hover a cell for the position + value

Functional annotation - snpEff impact + effect classes per sample; the missense/silent ratio is a pN/pS PROXY, not a selection test

Functional gene burden - genes carrying the most HIGH+MODERATE variants across the cohort, with the dominant effect class; a functional companion to Variable genes below, not a selection test

Variable genes - genes/regions with the most SNPs across the cohort; click a row to mark it on the SNP track

SNP dynamics - search & select genes to see the allele-frequency trajectories of their variants over time; events flag emergence / fixation / loss / non-synonymous

Epistasis - pairs of variants whose allele-frequency trajectories move together (concordant) or in opposition (discordant) within a connected series; candidate linked / co-selected / competing SNPs

SNP matrix - every SNP site (rows) × sample (columns); each cell is the allele frequency (hover for AF & depth), a striped cell = not called. Filter by gene / position, then download the full matrix as a TSV.

Drug resistance - resistance-associated mutations detected by pathotypr (WHO catalogue, H37Rv numbering). A genomic screen, NOT a clinical result; grade 1–2 = associated with resistance, 3 = uncertain, 4–5 = not associated.

aDNA damage authentication - terminal deamination per ancient sample; a screen, not a proof of authenticity

Temporal sampling overview - per-lineage year span parsed from dates + an informative-site proxy; a readiness check for downstream time-resolved analysis, NOT a clock estimate

sample yearbar = sampling spaninformative-site proxy = median SNPs

Selection: pN/pS and dN/dS - alignment-based per-gene dN/dS from eskaks; a cohort selection screen, not a per-sample QC metric
